Privacy for Parents

Folio Junior is the kid-safe side of Folio — where your children write without the Shelf, the Guild, or anyone outside your family seeing their work by accident. This page tells you what we do and don't collect, and what rights you have.

Plain language, not legal advice. If you're onboarding non-family users or you need a formal compliance statement, talk to a solicitor. The facts below are accurate; the legal framing around them should be reviewed by someone qualified.

What a Junior profile is

A Junior is a profile that lives inside your Folio account. There's no separate child account, no child email, no child password. Your child "signs in" by tapping their tile on your device and typing a short PIN. If a stranger gets to your device, the PIN still has to be typed.

What we collect

  • First name, avatar, birth year, grade (if imported from Harbor). That's it. No surname, no address, no email, no phone.
  • The works your child writes. Stored in our database (Firebase, project miscellaneous-117e9) so they're there when your child opens Folio again on any device you've signed into.
  • Your approval decisions. Every time your child asks to share something and you approve or decline, that decision is recorded as an audit trail. We keep the audit so there's a provable record of parental consent.

What we never collect, under any circumstances

  • No analytics on Junior pages. No tracking pixels. No advertising. Ever.
  • No chat, no messaging, no comments.
  • No contact information for your child.
  • No data shared with third parties for advertising, profiling, or sale.

What stays off by default, and needs your approval per child

  • The Shelf. Juniors cannot publish to the public Shelf. They can only ask to share. You review the work and approve per folio. Approvals can be revoked at any time.
  • The Guild. Juniors cannot join the Guild at all in this version. (A supervised, moderated version is planned but not here yet.)
  • External share links, third-party exports, print orders, AI features. All off by default. You enable each one per child when you're ready.
  • Dictation (speech-to-text), when it ships. Browsers send audio to the vendor (Chrome → Google, Safari → Apple) for transcription. We never record or store the audio. You enable dictation per child only after reading this note.

Your rights

Export all of your child's data — any time. Folio Junior → Manage → Export (JSON). Includes the profile, every approval record, every folio. You can take it with you or just keep a backup.

Delete all of your child's data — any time. Folio Junior → Manage → Delete. Recursive and permanent: the profile, every folio owned by the Junior, and the approvals ledger are removed. Firestore cleanup is immediate from your view; backup retention follows our standard 30-day rolling backup policy.

Pause — temporarily disable a Junior without deleting their work.

Legal framing (summary)

GDPR (EU/UK/EEA). Ireland sets the digital age of consent at 16. Children under 16 cannot independently consent to processing of their personal data; you give that consent as the guardian when you create a Junior profile. Your consent is the lawful basis under Article 6(1)(a). Special-category data is not collected.

COPPA (United States). Children under 13 are covered; verifiable parental consent is required. Our model — the parent is the only authenticated user, the parent created the profile, the parent explicitly approves every data-sharing action — is intended to satisfy the "verifiable" standard, but see the warning at the top of this page about talking to a solicitor.
