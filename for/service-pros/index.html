How it works.

1.

List your service

Head to the Guild, sign in with Google, add your directory listing, then post an "Offering a service" post with your category, pitch, and rates. Takes five minutes. Free forever.

2.

Authors find you

Your post shows up in the marketplace filter. Authors browsing for your specific service see it. They can also reply to a "Seeking a service" post if their needs match what you offer.

3.

Get hired, paid directly

The author reaches out, you negotiate scope and rates like any freelance gig, they pay you directly. Folio doesn't touch the money and takes no cut. You keep 100% of what you charge.

The math is simple.
