Wattpad, Royal Road, Kindle Vella — every one of them owns your readers, takes a cut, or locks you in. Here's what serial writers switch to Folio for.

What you're leaving behind

  • The platform owns your readers. If they leave, you lose the list.
  • Ad-heavy reading experience. Your typography is fighting a banner.
  • Locked pricing tiers. Vella coins, Wattpad Paid Stories, take-it-or-leave-it splits.
  • KDP Select exclusivity. Can't sell wide while you're in the pool.
  • Cuts on every dollar. Amazon takes 30–65% before you see a cent.
  • No print path. Digital-only. When the series wraps, there's no book you can hold.

What Folio gives you

  • Your URL, your reader list. Every subscriber lives in your account, not a platform's.
  • Print-quality typography in every chapter, from the first draft forward.
  • Free serial, paid unlock, or tip jar — you set the price and Ko-fi/PayPal pays you direct.
  • Non-exclusive, always. Ship on Folio, keep your KDP wide, sell on your own site — no conflicts.
  • 0% commission on tips + sales. Every cent readers pay goes to you.
  • Print-ready PDF export when the series is done. Send to Lulu, IngramSpark, or your own printer.

Everything a serial actually needs.

Not a general blogging tool with chapters bolted on. Built from the ground up around the way serials release, reward, and grow.

📆

Schedule the drop

Set your cadence — daily, weekly, fortnightly. Folio unlocks the next chapter automatically and emails every subscriber the moment it goes live.

🎁

Bundle bonuses per episode

Drop an HTML5 mini-game, a bonus scene, art pack, or PDF translation alongside any chapter. Buyers get the running library; new bonuses appear the moment they're added.

💛

Tips + paid unlocks

Ko-fi tip button in the reader footer, or gate the full serial behind a paid unlock via Ko-fi / PayPal. Zero commission either way — you get 100% of what readers pay.

🤝

Affiliates who grow with you

Invite anyone to sell your serial for a cut you set. Each affiliate gets a link, a QR code, and a live earnings ledger. Perfect for translators, bloggers, or a friend with a big audience.

📖

Real print, when it's time

When the series wraps, export a print-ready PDF with proper typography, chapter ornaments, running headers, and page numbers. Send it to Lulu or IngramSpark.

🔗

Google finds your book

Every published folio gets its own SEO landing page (onfolio.press/f/<your-slug>) with rich metadata, so readers searching for your title actually find it.
