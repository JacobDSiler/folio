# Folio Affiliate Program — Terms

**Last updated:** August 2026
**Applies to:** all affiliate arrangements made through Folio's Ship › Affiliates flow.

This is a short plain-language agreement. Owner and affiliate both accept it when they set up or accept an affiliation.

---

## What this program is

An owner of a Folio can invite anyone to help sell it in exchange for a percentage of each attributed sale. Folio provides the tracking infrastructure — invite flow, unique affiliate link, attribution cookie, ledger, and email notifications. Folio does **not** hold, process, or transmit the affiliate's commission payments. Payment is a direct arrangement between owner and affiliate, made outside Folio (typically via Ko-fi or PayPal).

## What the owner agrees to

1. **Pay the commission you set.** When Folio's ledger shows an amount owed to an affiliate, you agree to pay it within a reasonable window (30 days is customary). If a rate is edited later, existing sales keep the rate that applied at the time of sale.
2. **Pay directly and promptly.** Use the affiliate's stated Ko-fi or PayPal handle, or an alternative you and the affiliate agree on. Mark payments as settled in Folio once sent so both ledgers stay in sync.
3. **Provide accurate handles + emails.** Keep your contact information current so affiliates can reach you.
4. **Handle refunds fairly.** If a sale you're owed refunds and the affiliate has already been paid, note the reversal in your next settlement or reach out to the affiliate directly. Folio surfaces refund clawbacks on the owner dashboard.
5. **Tax reporting is your responsibility.** Because Folio never holds affiliate money, Folio does not issue tax forms. If your affiliate is in a jurisdiction that requires you to issue a 1099-NEC or equivalent for annual payments over a threshold, that's your responsibility as the payer.

## What the affiliate agrees to

1. **Only claim your own referrals.** The affiliate cookie is meant to attribute organic sales that came through your link. Don't self-attribute (buying your own copy through your link) or stuff cookies through unrelated content.
2. **Represent the Folio honestly.** Don't misrepresent the book's contents, use false scarcity, or make claims the owner hasn't approved. If in doubt, ask the owner.
3. **No spam, no manipulation.** Don't post your affiliate link into forums, chats, or channels where you're not welcome. Don't buy fake traffic or use bots. Don't attempt to bypass or falsify the attribution system.
4. **Provide payout handles.** Keep your Ko-fi and/or PayPal.me handles current on your dashboard so the owner can pay you.
5. **You are not an employee of the owner or of Folio.** You're an independent affiliate. You're responsible for reporting your income to your tax authority.

## What Folio agrees to

1. **Track the ledger accurately.** Attribution, commission calculation, and settlement history reflect the actual events recorded by the paywall worker at sale time.
2. **Not take a cut.** Folio takes 0% of tips and paid-release revenue. The full commission goes from owner to affiliate.
3. **Not act as a payment processor.** Folio provides the ledger; the payment itself is between owner and affiliate.
4. **Not resolve disputes.** If an owner and affiliate disagree about a payment, Folio provides the ledger record and steps back. You'll need to resolve the disagreement between yourselves.

## Termination

Either side can end an affiliation at any time:

- The **owner** can pause (stop new attribution, ledger stays) or remove (soft delete, history preserved) an affiliate from the Ship › Affiliates panel.
- The **affiliate** can stop sharing their link at any time. To be formally removed from an owner's ledger, message them directly and ask them to remove you.
- Removing an affiliation does not cancel any commission the owner still owes at that point. Settle up before parting ways.

## Prohibited content

The affiliate program is not available for Folios containing:

- Sexual content involving minors
- Real-world gore, violence, or filth
- Content that violates Folio's Community Standards

If Folio removes a folio for violating these rules, all affiliations tied to that folio are terminated. Any commissions already earned remain the owner's obligation to pay.

## Changes to these terms

Folio may update these terms. Material changes will be emailed to active owners and affiliates. Continued use of the program after a change constitutes acceptance.
