# Folio — marketing copy

Two pieces here: a short **blurb** for indie authors, and a scannable
**feature list** organized by what an author actually wants to do.
Both are ready to drop onto the `/press/` page, a landing page, an
email, or a social post — trim and reshuffle as needed.

## Feature list — organized by author outcome

The pricing page and marketing surfaces should lead with what the
author *gets to do*, not with technology names. Grouped that way
below.

### Write

- Rich chapter-based editor with drag-to-reorder, live word count,
  find & replace with highlight-in-context, auto-save, and version
  history (manual snapshots + automatic ones behind a collapsible
  panel).
- Import from `.docx`, `.txt`, `.md`, or `.rtf`. Auto-detects
  chapters using smart heuristics or Markdown headings; manual
  split view for tricky manuscripts.
- Write or paste directly into the app if you don't have a doc yet.
- Multi-language folios: write the same book in several languages
  side by side, with per-chapter language toggle in the editor and
  a language switcher for readers.
- Full manuscript view for anything longer than a chapter (a whole-
  book preview mode).
- Front matter, back matter, chapters, and table of contents as
  first-class section types with proper pagination.
- Cloud sync across devices + local file-system backup so nothing
  is ever lost.

### Design

- Professional typesetting engine that paginates in real time as
  you type — you always see the book as it will print or export.
- Page sizes: Trade 6x9, Pocket 5x8, Digest 5.5x8.5, A4, US Letter,
  or a custom size in inches or millimetres.
- Typography: body fonts, chapter title fonts, custom line spacing,
  paragraph indent style, justification, drop caps, ornaments,
  running headers and footers, page numbers.
- Cover images, chapter images, inline images in three sizes (plus
  full-bleed page layouts).
- Character-dialogue highlighting: assign a colour to each speaker
  and their dialogue gets a subtle tint throughout the book.
- Dark mode + light mode preview.

### Publish

- One-click publish to a shareable link that opens straight into
  the reader — no separate PDF to email around.
- Publish to the Shelf (Folio's public catalog) or keep it private
  and share the link only with the people you choose.
- Reader roles: reader, beta reader, editor — control who can see
  what, and who can leave annotations.
- Signed teaser links for social sharing (a single chapter opens
  publicly; the rest stays locked).
- Serial releases: schedule chapters to unlock over days, weeks,
  or months. Subscribers get an email when each chapter drops.
- Paid releases via Gumroad — set a price, readers unlock the
  whole folio after purchase, you keep 100% of the revenue.
- Print-on-demand via Lulu (order proofs, sell hardcopies).
- Export to PDF, EPUB, MOBI, DOCX, RTF, Markdown, HTML, or plain
  text at any time.

### Grow

- Custom imprint page — a public author profile at your own vanity
  URL that showcases your folios, your bio, and your customized
  brand.
- Newsletter subscribers per folio — readers opt in to hear about
  updates and future chapters.
- Featured Boosts — promote a folio to the top of the Shelf for
  24 hours to a week; comes with a 20% discount on Indie and 50%
  off on Imprint.
- Reviews + testimonials: readers rate and review your work;
  approved reviews display on your Shelf listing and imprint page.
- 24-hour free Featured Boost every time you submit a review of
  someone else's folio (pay it forward, get discovered).
- Author annotations: leave margin notes for readers, or invite
  editors to leave notes for you.

### Track

- Author dashboard with per-folio metrics — views, subscribers,
  reviews, annotations, and live publish state.
- Indie tier: 30-day view sparkline + per-chapter drop-off chart
  so you can see where readers stop reading.
- Imprint tier: top countries + top referrers so you know where
  to invest marketing.
- Platform metrics dashboard for admins (site-wide health, revenue,
  moderation queue).

### Earn

- Tips at 0% commission — Folio never touches the money, it goes
  straight to you.
- Paid releases via Gumroad — set your own price, keep 100% after
  Gumroad's own processing fees.
- Founding contributor badge for early Press subscribers, visible
  on your imprint page.
- Comp system for testing, family + friends, accessibility, or
  beta partners (admin-managed).
