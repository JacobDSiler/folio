# Video 1 — "Publish your first folio in 5 minutes" (TRIMMED to 3:30)

**Replaces** `VIDEO_1_HERO_SCRIPT.md` for production. The original 5-7 min version is kept in the repo for reference; this is the shoot-this-one cut.

**Target length:** 3:30 (matches the length advertised on `/help/tutorials/`).
**Audience:** A brand-new user who has never seen Folio.
**Primary purpose:** The hero slot on the tutorials page + homepage/social embeds.
**Tier:** 1 — ONBOARDING.
**Trimmed from v1 by:** cutting the leisurely cold open, the chapter-edit-in-place demo (visually confusing on video), and the Ink handoff shot (belongs in a dedicated Ink tutorial).

---

## Verified flow (pilot-checked on live site 2026-10-14)

1. Homepage → **Start writing — free** → opens `/app.html`
2. Default sample folio "The Midnight Garden" by Eleanor Ashford loads with a preview on the right
3. Sign in with Google via the toolbar (top-right Sign in link uses `?signin=1` and auto-kicks the popup) OR via the Ship/Release tab's "Connect Google account" prompt
4. Replace sample content by dragging a `.docx/.txt/.md/.rtf` into the import drop zone in the Write tab (auto-splits chapters)
5. Switch to **Design** tab → upload cover
6. Click toolbar **🔖 Release** (opens the release modal — same panel as Ship → ⚙ Settings → Release)
7. Fill: **Access** (default: 🌐 Public & free) → Description → tick **List on the Folio Shelf** → pick a content rating → tick the **Required to release** attestation
8. Click the modal's **🔖 Release** button
9. Share link box appears — copy, test by opening in a new tab (reader view)

## Voiceover script (3:30 at ~145wpm)

Delivered warm, conversational — NPR podcast register, not corporate.

> *(0:00)* You have a story. Folio is the fastest way to turn it into a real book you can share at a real URL — in your browser, free, no software to install.
>
> *(0:12)* Head to onfolio.press and click **Start writing**. You land in the editor with a short sample called "The Midnight Garden" so you can see how everything renders before you paste your own in.
>
> *(0:28)* Click **Sign in with Google** so your folios are saved to your account and available from any computer. Takes a second. No credit card.
>
> *(0:40)* Now your own writing. Drag a Word doc or a plain-text file into this import zone and Folio auto-splits it into chapters. Or click any paragraph in the preview and type.
>
> *(1:05)* Switch to the **Design** tab. Pick a theme, choose an accent colour, upload a cover. If you don't have cover art yet, Folio generates a tasteful placeholder from your title.
>
> *(1:35)* Ready? Hit the **🔖 Release** button in the top right. This is where your folio becomes a real book that other people can read.
>
> *(1:52)* Pick an access mode — Public and free is the default. Add a short description. If you want the book on Folio's public **Shelf** so readers can discover it, tick that box. Pick a content rating. And confirm the work is yours — that's required to release.
>
> *(2:35)* Click Release. Your folio is live. Here's the share link. Copy it, send it to anyone.
>
> *(2:52)* Here's what your reader sees — a clean reading page, chapters, bookmarks, dark mode. Works on any phone, tablet, or laptop. No app to install, no account required to read.
>
> *(3:12)* That's it. You've written a book, made it beautiful, and shared it with the world. **No commission. No walled garden. onfolio.press — free, forever.**
