# Testimonial seed outreach

**Purpose:** unblock the hidden "Real words from real authors" section on `onfolio.press` by getting 2-3 legitimate reviews from writer contacts. The welcome page currently hides the section when 0 approved reviews exist — one seed puts the block back on the page.

**How reviews land on the welcome page:**

1. The person visits any published folio you own (e.g. `onfolio.press/f/embers`).
2. In the reader, they scroll to the footer and click **★ Leave a review**.
3. They rate + write a paragraph and submit.
4. You approve it from `/admin/reviews/` — set `approvedForDisplay = true` and `allowMarketing = true`. (Both are required for it to appear on the welcome page.)
5. Refresh `onfolio.press` — the section unblocks with their words.

**Best first asks (in order of "most likely to say yes + most credible on the page"):**

- **Thomas** — he's been closest to the tool, and a review from him carries the "real author on Folio" weight the page needs.
- **Ken** — even a short "I picked this to distribute a translation for a reason" line lands well next to a real name and a folio he's tied to.
- **One other early writer** you trust — someone who's actually used the editor for more than 5 minutes and had a positive reaction.

Skip Reedsy-type marketplace pings — you want people who can honestly speak to Folio, not paid opinions.

Copy, tweak the name + specific detail, send:

> Hey [Name] — quick ask.
>
> I'm seeding real testimonials on the Folio welcome page (currently hidden because there are zero approved reviews and I refuse to fake it). Would you write a 2-3 sentence review of Folio if you've had a chance to use it?
>
> If yes, easiest path: [open my folio at `onfolio.press/f/<your-slug>`], scroll to the footer, hit ★ Leave a review, drop your rating and paragraph. Approvals go through me before anything publishes — I'll only push it to the marketing page if you tick "OK to use publicly" (which you can leave off if you'd rather it just be a private thumbs-up).
>
> Zero pressure. If Folio hasn't clicked for you, honest silence is fine. If it has and you can spare five minutes, this genuinely moves the needle.
>
> Thanks either way.
>
> — Jacob

## What to look for in a good testimonial

Approve the ones that:

- **Are specific.** "The chapter detection just worked on my messy .docx" beats "great product."
- **Sound like a person.** Not marketing-speak.
- **Land a clear thing Folio does differently.** 0% commission, all-in-one, print-quality preview, no software install.

Skip (or ask for a revision on) ones that:

- Read as generic praise ("amazing tool!")
- Praise you personally rather than the product
- Are so long the excerpt would need cutting

Two to three specific, human ones are worth more than ten generic ones.

## After the first three land

Once the section is unblocked and visible, mention it to every new user in the welcome email — they've just landed, they're seeing the tool for the first time, they might have the same reaction and be willing to say so. The welcome email itself doesn't need to ask — but a small "share your Folio experience →" link in the editor footer would catch a percentage.

**Follow-up task after seeding:** consider a small "leave a review of Folio itself" link in the editor sidebar CTA bar (near the "Support Folio" Ko-fi link) that opens a lightweight review composer scoped to the Folio-as-a-product, not a specific folio. Not needed for the seed round.
