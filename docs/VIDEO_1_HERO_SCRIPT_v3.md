**Voice recommendation for ElevenLabs:** `Rachel` (warm, mid-range, works for both female-voiced reference and later female-voiced final) or `Charlotte` (slightly younger, more inviting). Avoid anything too newsreader-formal.

**Punctuation to pacing:**
- Period = short pause (~0.3s)
- Em-dash = thinking pause (~0.5s)
- Paragraph break (blank line) = long breath (~1s) — ElevenLabs honours these when "Stability" is set 0.4-0.5

## ╔ Narration ╗

You have a story. Maybe it's been living in a notebook, or in a folder somewhere on your laptop, or in your head for years. Folio is the fastest way to turn it into a real book that readers can open at a real URL — in your browser, with nothing to install.

Head to onfolio.press and click Start writing. You'll land in the editor, with a short sample called The Midnight Garden already open — just so you can see how everything renders before you paste your own writing in.

First thing — sign in with Google. This saves your folios to your account, so they're available from any device. One click. No credit card. If you'd rather work as a guest, you can — but your work only lives on this one browser.

Now, your own writing. If you have a Word document, a plain text file, or a Markdown file, just drag it into the import area in the sidebar. Folio will split it into chapters for you, automatically.

Or, if you'd rather, click any paragraph in the preview on the right and type directly. Folio autosaves as you write. Nothing to remember. Nothing to lose.

Switch over to the Design tab. This is where your book becomes a real book. Pick a theme that matches the mood of your writing. Choose an accent colour — chapter breaks and headers will inherit it, so the whole book feels of a piece.

Upload a cover image. If you don't have cover art ready yet, Folio generates a tasteful placeholder from your title and author name, so you always have something to share.

Ready? Hit the Release button in the top right. This is where your folio becomes a real book that other people can read.

Pick an access mode. Public and free is the default — anyone with the link can open it. Choose Paid if you want to sell it, or Private link if you'd rather share it with just a few people.

Add a short description — a line or two that tells readers what they're about to pick up. If you want your folio to appear on Folio's public Shelf, where readers browse by genre and mood, tick that box. Pick a content rating. And confirm the work is yours — that last one's required.

Click Release. Your folio is live. Here's your share link — copy it, send it to a reader, post it anywhere.

Here's what your reader sees. A clean reading page. Chapters along the side. Bookmarks. Dark mode for the late nights. It works on any phone, tablet, or laptop. No app to install. No account required to read.

That's it. You've written a book, made it beautiful, and shared it with the world. No commission on your sales. No walled garden.

onfolio.press. Write and publish. Keep what you earn.
