# Series 01 — Your first Folio, published

**Version:** 2 (rewrite against current UI, Aug 2026)
**Target length:** 4:00 spoken, 4:15 with breathing room
**Reader guidance:** Warm, unhurried. Pause a beat between beats. If a beat feels tight, slow down — the visuals will speed-ramp to match you, not the other way around.

---

## Cold open (0:00 – 0:12)

> This is Folio. If you have a manuscript sitting on your computer, I'm going to walk you through turning it into a published book that anyone can read from a link. It takes about five minutes. Let's go.

**On-screen:** Landing page at `onfolio.press`. Static hold.

---

## Sign in (0:12 – 0:28)

> Head to onfolio dot press. Click **Sign in with Google** in the top right, pick your account, and you'll land in the Folio editor.

**On-screen:** Cursor to Sign in with Google → account chooser → editor loads.

---

## The editor at a glance (0:28 – 0:48)

> Everything about your book lives on this one page. Across the top you'll see four tabs — **Write**, **Design**, **Produce**, and **Ship**. On the left is where you build your book. On the right is the live preview — what your readers will see.

**On-screen:** Slow cursor sweep across the four top tabs, then across the left panel, then across the right preview.

---

## Import your manuscript (0:48 – 1:18)

> Start on the **Write** tab. Find the **Import** panel, and drag your Word document straight onto the drop zone. Folio reads the file, and in a second or two you'll see a message that says **Detected: however many chapters**. If that number looks right, click **Split into chapters** and your book is loaded.

**On-screen:** Write tab active → drag `.docx` onto drop zone → "Detected: N chapters" appears → click **Split into chapters** → chapter list populates on the left, preview updates on the right.

---

## Fix detection if it's wrong (1:18 – 1:38)

> If Folio missed a chapter break, no problem — there's a **detection mode** dropdown right there. Switch it to **Heading 1**, or **Page break**, or **Manual**, and try again. You can also open the manuscript in split view and mark chapters by eye.

**On-screen:** Open the detection mode dropdown, hover the options, close it. No re-import needed.

---

## Design your book (1:38 – 2:10)

> Now hop over to the **Design** tab. This is where your book gets its look. Set your **page size** — Trade six-by-nine is the classic paperback, Digest is a little smaller, and A4 or Letter is nice for a manual or workbook. Then pick a **body font**. Cycle through a few and watch the preview update on the right until you find one that fits your voice.

**On-screen:** Design tab → open page-size dropdown, hover Trade 6×9, Digest, A4, close on Trade 6×9 → click through 2-3 body fonts, land on Playfair Display or Garamond.

---

## Title, author, and metadata (2:10 – 2:35)

> Still on the **Design** tab, scroll to **Book Details**. Type your **title**, type your **author name**, and add any subtitle, series, or ISBN if you have them. This is what shows on your cover, on the reader page, and on the Folio Shelf.

**On-screen:** Book Details section → type title → type author → briefly highlight the other fields.

---

## Save your Folio (2:35 – 2:58)

> When you're happy, click the **Ship** tab and pick the **Folio** sub-tab. Give your Folio a short web-friendly name — this becomes part of your reader link — and click **Save**. You'll get a green confirmation and your custom URL appears just below.

**On-screen:** Ship tab → Folio sub-tab → type slug (e.g. `the-kept-hours`) → click Save → toast → URL displays.

---

## Publish (2:58 – 3:30)

> Ready to go live? Click the big green **Publish** button. A modal opens with your visibility choices — **Public and free**, **Paid**, or **Private link only**. Pick one. If you want your book listed on the public Folio Shelf where readers browse, tick **List on the Folio Shelf**. Then click **Release this Folio**.

**On-screen:** Click Publish → modal opens → hover the three visibility options, click Public and free → tick the shelf checkbox → click Release this Folio → success toast.

---

## Share the link (3:30 – 3:52)

> That's it — you're published. Click **Copy link** to grab your reader URL, or hit **Preview as reader** to see exactly what your audience will see. Paginated pages, running header, page numbers, the works.

**On-screen:** Click Copy link → toast confirms copy → click Preview as reader → reader loads, scroll a page or two, cursor touches header + page number.

---

## Metrics (3:52 – 4:08)

> When you want to check how your book is doing, come back to **Ship** and click the **Metrics** sub-tab. Views, subscribers, reviews, annotations — it's all here. Hit refresh any time to pull the latest numbers.

**On-screen:** Back to editor → Ship → Metrics sub-tab → cursor hovers each tile → click refresh.

---

## Outro (4:08 – 4:20)

> You've got a Folio. It's live, it's shareable, and it's yours. In the next tutorials I'll show you how to charge for it, add audio, run a serial release, and build a following on the Shelf. See you there.

**On-screen:** Wide editor view with the published-state chrome visible (green Published badge, folio name in title bar). Static hold.

---

## Notes for the reader

- **Total spoken length target:** 4:00 – 4:15. If you naturally land closer to 4:30 that's fine — visuals will absorb it.
- **Pace:** Warm and unhurried. Think "audiobook narrator explaining a hobby," not "commercial voiceover."
- **Pronunciations:**
  - "Folio" — FOH-lee-oh
  - ".docx" — say "dot doc-ex"
  - "onfolio.press" — "on-FOH-lee-oh dot press"
- **Emphasis words** are in **bold** in the script — a gentle lift, not a stab.
- **Pauses:** the paragraph breaks are natural breath points. Use them.

## Notes for the shot script

- Every beat has a clear on-screen anchor. No beat requires the visual to hit a precise sub-second frame.
- If a beat runs long in the take, the video editor slows the visual segment; if a beat runs short, we speed-ramp gently.
- Cursor movement should be **deliberate**. First-time users watch the cursor. Never blink between clicks.
- The **Publish** modal is the visual hero moment. Give it 3-4 seconds of settle before the click.

## Changes vs. v1

- Tabs renamed: Manuscript → **Write**, Book → **Design**, Folio (cloud icon) → **Ship > Folio**, Metrics → **Ship > Metrics**.
- New buttons referenced: **Split into chapters**, **Release this Folio**, **Preview as reader**, **Copy link**.
- New copy referenced: "Detected: N chapters" indicator, "List on the Folio Shelf" checkbox, three-way visibility (Public and free / Paid / Private link).
- Length budget stretched from 3:30 to 4:00-4:15 so the reader isn't fighting the clock.
- Removed the "cloud icon" wayfinding — the Ship tab is labeled and doesn't need visual hinting.
