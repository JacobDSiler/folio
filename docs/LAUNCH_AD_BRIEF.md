# Folio launch ad brief

**Problem to solve:** No one's floating in the tool watching the
tutorials. The tutorials are instrumental, but they're bottom-of-
funnel content — they need top-of-funnel traffic to be useful.

**Short answer:** Run small-budget Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads
targeting independent authors with a 10-second video that opens on
"your book, published, free, 0% commission" and ends with the
onfolio.press URL. Spend $100-200 over 2 weeks. Track signups.

## Who to target

**Narrow audience, don't widen it.** Meta's auto-targeting burns
money on cold strangers. Hand-pick audiences by interest overlap:

- **Interest layer** (any one):
  - KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing)
  - Self-publishing
  - IngramSpark
  - "Writing a novel"
  - Scrivener (the writing app)
  - Reedsy
  - NaNoWriMo
  - Indie author community
- **Behaviour layer** (require):
  - "Engaged shoppers" OR
  - "Small business owners" OR
  - "Published author" page likes
- **Exclusion:**
  - People already following @folio_app or @onfolio (if the pages
    exist and have followers)
  - Age 13-17 (not your audience; cheaper CPM)

Geo: UK + US + Canada + Australia + Ireland + New Zealand. The
English-speaking author market is where your message lands.

## The ad itself — three variants to test

Spend $20-30 testing each in week 1, then pour budget into the one
with the lowest cost-per-signup for week 2.

### Variant A — the hero clip
A 10-second cut from the hero tutorial Allyson records: cold open +
cover shot + release button + reader page. No voiceover on the ad;
Instagram/TikTok silent-first autoplay is the dominant watch mode.

**On-screen text (burned in):**
```
Write your book.
Publish it.
Keep 100% of what you earn.
             ↳  onfolio.press — free
```

### Variant B — the "0% forever" claim
A static image (or 3-second video) of a book cover being generated
by Folio with the headline:

```
0% commission on your sales.
Not now. Not later. Zero.

onfolio.press
```

### Variant C — the testimonial-style
A 10-second talking-head from Jacob (or any one of your Press
subscribers who agreed) saying:

> "I published three novels on Folio in a weekend. Zero commission,
>  real royalties, no gatekeepers. It's at onfolio dot press."

## Budget plan

**Week 1 ($60-90):**
- $20-30 per variant × 3 variants = $60-90 total
- Daily cap: $10/day across all three
- Objective: "Traffic" (clicks to onfolio.press) OR "Leads" if
  you've got email capture on the homepage

**Week 2 ($100-120):**
- Kill the two worst-performing variants
- Pour the remaining ~$8/day into the winner for 10-14 more days
- Objective: switch to "Conversions" with onfolio.press/app visit
  as the conversion event (requires a Meta pixel on the homepage)

**Measure signups, not clicks.** CTR is vanity; signups are
revenue. Hook up Google Analytics or Plausible on onfolio.press and
tag UTM parameters on the ad URLs:

```
https://onfolio.press/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=launch-oct-2026&utm_content=hero-video
```

## Not-Meta alternatives if the above feels like too much

1. **Reddit ads on r/selfpublish, r/writing, r/KDP** — same budget,
   same video, way less targeting setup. $50 for a 2-day test.
2. **Twitter/X boosted post** — if Jacob already has a presence,
   boost a thread about Folio's build journey for $30.
3. **ProductHunt launch** — free, one-shot, can drive 500-5000
   visitors on a good day. Costs you the submission effort + a
   couple of hours of launch-day engagement.
4. **Author newsletters** — pay $50-200 to be featured in a
   newsletter aimed at indie authors (e.g. The Hot Sheet, Written
   Word Media). Highest-intent audience of any option.

## My recommendation if you want to ship FAST

**Do the ProductHunt launch first** — zero cash out of pocket, and
if it goes well you'll have your first wave of signups watching the
tutorials within 24 hours. It'll also tell you if your value prop
lands (comments give you honest cold-user reactions). After PH,
spend $50-100 on Reddit to validate what messaging actually clicks,
THEN scale up on Meta with the proven winner.

ETA for ProductHunt launch readiness: ~2 days of prep (gallery
images, maker comment, scheduled launch time, inviting a dozen
friends to upvote in the first hour).

## Not now, but keep in mind

- SEO is the long game. Make sure `onfolio.press` ranks for
  "free self-publishing" and "0% commission book platform" — add a
  landing page per keyword, publish the Press blog if you have one.
- Partnerships. Reach out to indie-author YouTubers (Alyssa Matesic,
  iWriterly, etc.) and offer them a Perpetual Imprint tier in
  exchange for an honest review video. Zero cash, high intent
  traffic.
- Guild community itself becomes marketing. Every author on the
  Guild who promotes their own book links back to Folio.
